# About Sabita Karki

I am a management researcher specializing in artificial intelligence, social commerce, digital marketing, and consumer behavior. My work examines how AI-enabled technologies influence purchase decisions, trust, perceived value, perceived risk, and repurchase intention.

I integrate technology-adoption theories, behavioral models, and quantitative research methods to investigate AI-driven business transformation, with particular attention to digital commerce and service industries in emerging economies.

Alongside research, I bring more than nine years of university teaching, academic leadership, curriculum development, and student research supervision across management and marketing disciplines.

## Current Roles

- Ph.D. Researcher, Faculty of Management, Tribhuvan University
- Adjunct Faculty, Kathmandu Model College
- Team Leader, DNS Web
