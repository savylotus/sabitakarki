# Research

My research investigates human decision-making around AI-enabled commerce and services using behavioral theory and quantitative methods.

## Doctoral Research

**Topic:** *AI-Driven Social Commerce Features and Consumer Purchase Decisions for Fashion Apparel: The Mediating Role of Trust, Perceived Value, and Perceived Risk*

The project develops and tests a consumer purchase-decision model for AI-supported social commerce. It draws on technology-adoption and behavioral frameworks and uses PLS-SEM and survey-based quantitative analysis.
